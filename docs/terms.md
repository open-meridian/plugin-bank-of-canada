# Terms

A summary, not legal advice: read the Bank's own words before relying on
them. Terms change; these were read on 18 November 2026.

From the Bank of Canada's terms ([bankofcanada.ca/terms](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/terms/)):

- **Use with credit.** The Bank's website content may be used, copied and
  distributed with credit to the Bank and a note of any changes, implying no
  endorsement by the Bank.
- **Free on the Bank's website.** A paid service that includes it must tell
  its buyers that it is available free on the Bank's website.
- **Third parties' content** is excluded unless marked.
- **Request limits** may not be circumvented, Valet's included.
- **Exchange rates** are not meant as a benchmark for executing foreign
  exchange trades.

Each dataset describes these terms in words, shown on the Data sources
page, and declares no licence. The licence a deployment admin sets there is
the one enforced; the plugin says nothing of whether your deployment meets
anyone's terms.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Bank of Canada.
